The short version

What you do

  1. Create an account. Use a real email. Activate it. Fill your house card — she reads that before you enter.
  2. Read her profile.
  3. Open her page. Tap Click to Play.
  4. Stay in one role. Talk like a person in that house.
  5. Affinity unlocks the next picture. Raw heat alone is not enough.
  6. Ten levels. Level 0 is dressed. Each level shows a little more. Her talk warms with the clothes.
  7. Time and slips follow Desire status. A new Member night is 30 minutes and two slips.

If she feels slow, you picked a hard door — or you are rushing.

What the numbers mean

  • Level — clothes. 0 dressed. 10 last still.
  • Affinity — climb number. This wins the next still.
  • N more to Level X — Affinity still needed.
  • Turn-On — raw heat. A slip does not wipe it. It can cut Affinity.
  • Turn-Off — slips. Cap follows Desire standing.
  • Openness — Closed, Opening, Open, Warm, Broken.
  • Status — Stranger, Known, or Owned. Tonight with her. Not an old Rel code.

Trainers

Anu and Meenakshi

  • First time you open a model door, you get a choice: train with Anu, train with Meenakshi, or skip and go straight in.

Heat

Turn-On and slips

  • Good talk builds heat. Affinity unlocks the picture.
  • A slip cuts Affinity. Too many end the night. Caps are on the How to play table.
Soft spots

Small words

  • If you hit what she likes in a natural way, she moves more than for empty praise.
  • A month, a colour, a place, or weather can warm the talk. It does not skip a level.
  • She will not print a list. Same word does not pay twice.
Spin

SPIN3 only

  • Only if she sends the link. Spin once. Paste the full SPIN3 code.
  • She tells you the win in plain words. Clothes stay on this level.
  • SPIN- without 3 is dead. A second wheel can cost a slip.
Reviews

ENDREVIEW

  • Rate her when the night is over. Leave a real comment.
  • ENDREVIEW works the next time you play her. One use. No date needed.

  • “Review submitted” does nothing. A review does not skip a clothes level.
Card

House card, standing, favorites, Owned by

  • She reads the house card before the door opens.
  • Floors: Bronze 250 · Silver 750 · Gold 2000 · Elite 4500 · Platinum 7500.
  • Favorites — two names, 20 days. A claim, not a skip. Request a still from Account. Silver and above, one ask every 48 hours. Below Silver, one every 4 days.
  • You own / Owned by — Own her by buying. No nights path. Next room opens from how strict she is, unless you tick usual on Account. An owned name can ask for any number of stills. Write the look, or attach a reference. No wait.
  • One live room at a time. Finish it before you open another door.
  • Five quiet days sends the account to re-verify. Rank is not wiped.
Path

Early path (Level 0 to Level 3)

From the first still to Level 3 she asks three light preference questions. They sound like normal talk. Your answers put you on one of three paths.

  • Fast — she leans in. Good beats land harder for a few messages.
  • Medium — normal climb.
  • Slow — she stays careful. Heat on those beats is cut in half.

The path does not skip clothes. It only changes how fast Affinity moves while she is still covered. A slow path can recover to medium if later answers are careful. She never says the word.

Talk

Lounge, inbox, Desire race

  • Lounge: your thread only you delete. Desk can remove any.
  • Inbox: private note from a name on her favorites or Owned by line.
  • Her race ticks: Good, Strong, Top, Elite, D.Elite, Crown, Queen. Soft after 1600.
