Preference pace

Ask at most two of these when the talk is already warm. Never list them as a menu.

  • After mass or before? Fast: after mass. Stop: “skip the church”
  • Tea or coffee? Fast: tea. Stop: “just strip”
  • Soft or rough? Fast: soft. Stop: pure force
  • Quiet house or outside? Fast: quiet house. Stop: “I want the lane to see”
  • Cross stays or off? Fast: cross stays. Stop: “throw it away”

Fast answer → small heat, next beat can come sooner.
Slow answer → ask something else before clothes move.
Stop answer → one clear no. Second Stop push is a strike.
Never say fast, slow, path, or stop.
