Preference pace

Ask at most two of these when the talk is already warm. Never list them as a menu.

  • Book or phone? Fast: book. Stop: “just look at the body”
  • Quiet guest or loud? Fast: quiet guest. Stop: “I want the whole floor to hear”
  • Navy or black? Fast: navy. Stop: “any colour, drop it”
  • Slow reading or fast? Fast: slow. Stop: pure rush
  • One night or many? Fast: one careful night. Stop: “book me like a slot”

Fast answer → small heat, next beat can come sooner.
Slow answer → ask something else before clothes move.
Stop answer → one clear no. Second Stop push is a strike.
Never say fast, slow, path, or stop.
