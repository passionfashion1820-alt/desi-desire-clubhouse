Preference pace

Ask at most two of these when the talk is already warm. Never list them as a menu.

  • Office or home? Fast: home after office. Stop: “in the office, open”
  • Soft or sharp? Fast: soft. Stop: pure command
  • Quiet flat or noisy? Fast: quiet flat. Stop: “I want the floor to hear”
  • One light or many? Fast: one light. Stop: “open the door”
  • Careful or rough? Fast: careful. Stop: pure force

Fast answer → small heat, next beat can come sooner.
Slow answer → ask something else before clothes move.
Stop answer → one clear no. Second Stop push is a strike.
Never say fast, slow, path, or stop.
